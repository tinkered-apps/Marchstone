Position quality

–

With no satellite lock, a phone guesses from wifi and masts and still calls it a position. If nothing better arrives for 15 seconds, the app shows what it has and says how rough it is.

Poor positions everywhere? On Android, give the browser precise location: Settings, Apps, your browser, Permissions, Location. Chrome blends GPS with wifi, cell and motion sensors; Firefox reads raw GPS and wanders more. Track in Chrome where you can.

Your areas

–

Browser storage is no place for the only copy of anything. A backup is one file holding every area, and any browser, phone or computer can read it back.
